# Tasks

## Purpose
Manage daily tasks and long-term goals.

## Screens
- My tasks page with a quick-add bar, Filter, Sort, and Group menus, and two views: Cards (default) and List. Tasks are grouped into Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No date, and Completed (last 7 days, "Show more"). A third view, Board (kanban), has the columns To do, In progress and Done with the same cards. The Board shows the tasks that pass the filters, in the chosen sort order; dragging a card to another column changes its status (the Done column completes it). Drag and drop: a drag handle (grip) appears on a card or row when it is hovered or focused. Drag with the mouse (after moving 6 px, so a click still opens the task) or with the keyboard (Tab to the handle, Space lifts, arrow keys move, Space drops, Esc cancels; screen readers hear what is happening). Reordering inside a section needs Sort = Manual; otherwise one quiet line says "Sort is not Manual, so reordering is off." (moves between sections and columns still work). Drop on Today sets the due date to today (an existing time is kept, otherwise it becomes all-day). Drop on Upcoming opens a small date picker (it starts on tomorrow; Cancel or Esc puts the card back); the chosen day makes the task all-day, so a time of day is dropped. Overdue accepts no drops and No date only accepts reordering (a drop there shows a message). Drop on Completed completes the task; if the server refuses (for example a blocked task) the card goes back and the error toast shows the server's message. Dragging a completed card to another section reopens it. Every move shows an Undo toast ("Task moved."); undo restores the status, date and place of the moved task. Subtasks cannot be dragged, and nothing can be dragged in the Archived view (Cards, List, or Board). With Group set to Tag, Priority or None, cards can only be reordered inside their own group.
- Task detail panel (340 px): title, status (To do, In progress, Done), priority, due (date and time, or date only with an All day switch), reminder, repeat (a menu of presets) and blocked by (blocker chips and an Add blocker search), tags, subtasks (one level), notes, Archive or Unarchive, Delete.
- Archive: an "Archived" filter in the list view, plus archive and restore actions. There is no separate archive screen.
- Calendar view (Schedule-X) and the planning view (today, this week, this month) belong to the Calendar module (Step 4), not Step 2.

## Features
Create, edit, delete, subtasks, priorities 1–4, deadlines, reminders, recurring (RRULE), dependencies (blocks links), tags, filter, sort, archive. Searching tasks from the palette is planned (the filter menu covers status, priority, tag, due, and archived today).
Project link and category come in Step 5 (tags cover grouping until then).

## Rules
- Priority 1 is the most urgent, 4 the least (Urgent, High, Medium, Low). The default is 4.
- Dates are stored in UTC and shown and typed in India time (Asia/Kolkata, UTC+05:30). A due date can be all-day (date only, no time).
- The redesigned screens (side panel, Overview, My tasks) are described in docs/features/tasks-redesign.md.
- A reminder is only saved as a date for now. Notifications arrive with the reminder job.
- The quick-add bar understands what you type (India time): tags `#word` (not `C#` or a link), priority `!1` to `!4` or `!urgent`, `!high`, `!medium`, `!low` (the first one counts), repeats (`every day`, `daily`, `every week`, `weekly`, `every monday`, `every monday and thursday`, `every weekday`, `every month`, `every month on the 15th`, `every year`), and dates and times (`today`, `tomorrow`, `friday`, `next friday`, `in 3 days`, `oct 12`, `12 oct`, `5/11` day first, `5pm`, `tomorrow 5pm`, `at 9:30`). A date without a time is all-day; a time alone means the next time it happens (`5pm` typed at 6pm means tomorrow). A repeat needs a due date, so `every monday` alone sets the next Monday (today if it is Monday) and `every monday at 9am` sets that Monday at 9:00. Only the first date counts. A month name counts only with a number (`oct 12`, `12 oct`, `april 3`, `3 april 2026`; a lone April, June, August or Dec is just a word, and a day written in words is not read), a bare short weekday (mon, tue, wed, thu, fri) is ignored unless it follows on, next or this, and single ambiguous words (may, march, sat, sun, second, now, a) and years outside 2000 to 2100 are ignored. Slash dates are read day first, so `1/2 cup` becomes 1 February: click the x on the chip to keep the words. A tag name is up to 40 characters, the same tag typed twice counts once (ignoring capitals), a trailing . , ; : ! ? is not part of the name, `#` inside a word (C#, a link, a#b) is not a tag, and a name cannot hold invisible characters (zero-width space, text-direction marks); a task can have at most 20 tags (more gives the server error "A task can have at most 20 tags"). A repeat with no day uses the due date's weekday or day of the month; for the 29th to 31st, months without that day use their last day (so `every month on the 31st` typed on 1 November gives 30 November, the same rule the server uses for the next copies). Under the bar, small chips show what was understood; the x on a chip ("Ignore ...") puts those words back into the title. Enter creates the task with the title and only the understood fields; new tag names are created with the next palette color (and stay if you undo). A title is required: when only understood parts (date, tag, priority, repeat) are typed and no title, the send button is disabled and the bar says "Add a title". An ignored chip stays ignored while its words are in the box and is forgotten when they leave. When the box is focused and empty it suggests "Try: Pay rent every month #home !high".
- Favorites (Feature 5): a favorite is a saved My tasks view (status, priority, tags, due, archived, group, sort; not Cards/List/Board) with a name, a palette color, and an icon. In the side panel the "Favorites" header has a + button: "Save this view" (only on My tasks when a filter, sort or group differs from the default) or one of your tags (a tag favorite uses the tag's color). Rows are links (Enter opens), 32 px with a colored square; the row menu has Rename (or F2), Color and icon, Move up, Move down and Remove (or Delete, with an Undo toast "Favorite removed."); rows can also be dragged or lifted with Space. The collapsed panel shows only the squares with tooltips. At most 12 favorites ("12 favorites is the most. Remove one first."); saving a view that is already a favorite says "Already in favorites.". Overview shows the first 6 favorites as tiles with the open-task count and an overdue line (a favorite of archived or done tasks shows 0 there). Manage tags: "Manage tags…" at the end of the tag chip menu and of the Filter menu's Tag group opens a dialog with every tag A to Z (a search box above 8 tags): the color dot recolors, the pencil renames (a name that already exists asks "Merge 'house' into it?"), the trash deletes. Deleting a tag removes it from its tasks (they stay) and from favorites (a favorite left with nothing is removed); a tag that is used asks first; deleting and merging show an Undo toast (not offered above 5000 tasks). Undoing a merge brings the old tag back but the tasks keep the target tag. After a delete or merge the dialog also shows "Tag deleted." or "Tags merged." with an Undo button inside the dialog (for keyboard users; it goes away after 8 seconds), and focus moves to the next row. The undo of a delete or merge also gives the tag back to tasks that are in the trash. Restoring favorites by undo never goes above 12, skips favorites whose view would be a duplicate, and drops tag ids that no longer exist. A merge that makes two favorites identical removes the changed one (undo brings it back). Opening a favorite also resets Sort and Group to the My tasks defaults when the favorite has none. Moving a favorite with the menu says "Moved up." or "Moved down." to screen readers. If the Overview favorites cannot load, an error line with "Try again" shows.
- Subtasks go one level deep only.
- A blocked task (it has an unfinished task that blocks it) cannot be completed by any route (checkbox, status control, drag onto Done or Completed, the AI): the server answers 409 `blocked` with "Blocked by: ..." and the app shows that message and puts the task back. It shows a "Blocked" badge listing the blockers. In the detail panel, "Blocked by" lists the open blockers as chips with a remove button, or "Not blocked", and "Add blocker" opens a search of open tasks (not the task itself, subtasks, or tasks that already block it). A blocker that is done or deleted no longer blocks; an archived one still does. A circular dependency (A blocks B blocks A, or a longer chain) and a task blocking itself are refused. Adding and removing a blocker show an Undo toast.
- Any change of a task to done (the checkbox, the status control, a drag, the AI) creates the next copy of a recurring task, and its undo removes that copy. Its due date is the first occurrence of the RRULE that is after both the old due date and now (India time), so completing a task that is days overdue never creates a task that is already overdue. The copy keeps the time of day (an all-day task stays all-day), the title, description, priority, tags and reminder (moved by the same distance), starts as To do, goes after the last sibling, and has no subtasks. Monthly rules on the 29th to 31st use the last day of shorter months; a yearly rule on 29 February uses 28 February in other years (and then stays on the 28th, because the rule does not remember the 29th). Undoing the completion (the Undo toast) puts the task back and removes the copy, unless the copy was already completed. A repeating task needs a due date: clearing the due date in the detail panel also stops the repeat. Blocker links are not copied, so a new copy starts unblocked; if the task is done, reopened and done again, no second copy is made. In the detail panel, "Repeat" is a menu: Does not repeat, Every day, Every week (with weekday chips), Every month (with a day number, starting at the due date's day), Every year; it saves at once, and is disabled with "Set a due date to repeat" when there is no due date. A rule that is not one of the presets is shown as read-only text.
- Recurrence is chosen from presets (daily, weekly on chosen days, monthly, yearly). The rule is stored as an RRULE; there is no free-text rule field.
- Deleting a task is a soft delete. Complete, delete, and archive show an Undo toast ("Task completed.", "Task deleted.", "Task archived."); moves show "Task moved.", adding or removing a blocker shows "Blocker added." or "Blocker removed.". Reopening and unarchiving do not.
- Due dates read "Today, 5:00 PM", "Tomorrow", "Yesterday", "Mon, 13 Oct" (with the year when it is not this year); an all-day task shows no time. Overdue (red) means an earlier India day; a task due today at a time that has passed shows only its time in red.
- The UI calls GET /tasks, GET /tasks/:id, POST /tasks, PATCH /tasks/:id, POST /tasks/:id/complete, DELETE /tasks/:id, POST /tasks/:id/archive, POST /tasks/:id/unarchive, POST /tasks/:id/move, POST /tasks/:id/blockers, DELETE /tasks/:id/blockers/:blockerId, and POST /undo; priority changes go through PATCH.
- Wording: the stored status "doing" is shown as "In progress" everywhere (detail panel, cards, filter menu).
