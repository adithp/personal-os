# Calendar

## Purpose
Full scheduling with Google Calendar.

## Screens
Schedule-X views: day, week, month, agenda. Event detail panel. Calendar list with show/hide.

## Features
View, create, edit, delete events; recurring events; reminders; multiple calendars; link events to tasks and projects; tasks with due dates shown on the calendar.

## Sync
See docs/INTEGRATIONS.md.

## Actions
calendar.list, calendar.create, calendar.update, calendar.delete

## AI
- Daily planning: "What does my day look like today?" combines events, tasks, habits, and reminders.
- Scheduling: finds free time for tasks.

## Done when
I manage my calendar here and the AI plans my day.
