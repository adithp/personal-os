# Mail (Gmail)

## Purpose
Use Gmail without leaving the app.

## Screens
Inbox with labels, important filter, needs-reply filter; email reader; composer (new, reply, forward); search.

## Features
Read, send, reply, forward, search, draft, delete, archive, star, labels, attachments, important emails, filtering.

## Actions
gmail.search, gmail.read, gmail.draft, gmail.send, gmail.reply, gmail.forward, gmail.archive, gmail.star, gmail.label, gmail.delete

## AI
Summarize emails, draft replies, rewrite, identify important emails, find emails, extract tasks and deadlines, create tasks, add information to projects or notes. Sending always needs my confirmation.

## Done when
The AI answers "Check my emails and tell me what I need to respond to today."
